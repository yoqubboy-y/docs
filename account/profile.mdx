Your profile contains your personal information, contact details, and account preferences. Keeping your profile up to date ensures smooth communication and proper identification within your organization.

## Profile Information

Your profile contains the following information:

### Personal Details

**First Name**  
Your given name as it appears in the system.

**Last Name**  
Your surname or family name.

**Email**  
Your email address used for:
- Account authentication
- System notifications
- Communication from Rooler
- Integration connections (Email, Telegram)

<Note>
  Your email address is set by your administrator and typically cannot be changed directly. Contact your admin if you need to update your email.
</Note>

**Phone Number** (Optional)  
Your contact phone number for:
- Emergency communications
- SMS notifications (if enabled)
- Team contact information

**Date of Birth** (Optional)  
Used for identification and internal records.

**Timezone**  
Your local timezone ensures that all times and dates are displayed correctly for your location. This affects:
- Appointment times
- Load schedules
- Activity timestamps
- Report generation

<Tip>
  Setting the correct timezone is crucial for accurate scheduling and tracking, especially if your team works across multiple time zones.
</Tip>

### Visibility Settings

**Show Online Status**  
Control whether other users can see when you're online and active in Rooler.

- **Enabled** - Others can see when you're online
- **Disabled** - Your online status is hidden

## Editing Your Profile

To update your profile information:

1. Access your profile using either method above
2. Click on any field you want to edit
3. Enter your new information
4. Click **Save Changes** at the bottom

<Warning>
  Make sure all information is accurate, especially your email and phone number, as these are used for important communications.
</Warning>

## Changing Your Password

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Edit Email Address?

Your email address is typically set by your administrator and cannot be changed directly through your profile. If you need to update your email:

1. Contact your organization's administrator
2. They can update your email in the user management system
3. You'll receive new login credentials at your updated email

### Changes Not Saving?

If your profile changes aren't saving:

1. Ensure all required fields are filled out
2. Check that your information meets any format requirements
3. Verify you have a stable internet connection
4. Try refreshing the page and making changes again
5. Contact support if the issue persists

### Wrong Timezone Showing?

If times and dates appear incorrect:

1. Open your profile settings
2. Verify your timezone setting
3. Update to your correct local timezone
4. Save changes
5. Refresh the page to see corrected times
