## Why Rooler?

Running a carrier company shouldn't mean juggling 4+ applications. Traditional workflows force dispatchers and updaters to constantly switch between TMS, Google Sheets, Telegram, and Email—wasting time and energy.

**Rooler changes everything.** We've built the first true Logistics Operating System that unifies your entire workflow into a single, powerful interface.

## Core Features

Everything you need to run your carrier operations efficiently.

  <Accordion title="Boards & Analytics" icon="chart-line">

  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Management Modules" icon="sliders">
