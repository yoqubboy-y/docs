## Getting Started

Welcome to Rooler! This guide will walk you through the initial setup and core workflows.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive Your Invitation">
    Rooler uses an invitation-based system. You'll receive an email with:
    - Your login credentials
    - A temporary password
    
    <Warning>
      We strongly recommend changing your temporary password immediately after your first login for security.
    </Warning>
    
    <Info>
      If you forget your password later, visit our [Forgot Password](/account/forgot-password) section.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log In to Rooler">
    Navigate to [app.rooler.ai](https://app.rooler.ai) and log in with your credentials.
    
    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/quickstart/login.png" alt="Rooler login screen" />
    </Frame>
    
    After your first successful login, you'll be prompted to change your temporary password.
    
    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/quickstart/change_password_button.png" alt="Change password button location" />
    </Frame>
    
    Click the **Change Password** button to open the password change dialog.
    
    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/quickstart/change_password_dialog.png" alt="Change password dialog" />
    </Frame>
    
    Enter your current password and choose a new strong password that meets the requirements:
    - At least 8 characters
    - At least 1 number
    - At least 1 lowercase letter
    - At least 1 uppercase letter
  </Step>

  <Step title="Organization Setup">
    Your organization is set up by the Rooler team, but if you have **Owner** or **Admin** role permissions, you can:
    - Create new companies within your organization
    - Manage existing companies
    - Configure company settings
    
    Learn more in [Company Management](/management/company-management).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Integrations">
    Connect your essential tools to Rooler:
    - **Email** - Sync your Gmail, Outlook, or other email providers
    - **Telegram** - Connect your Telegram account for messaging
    - **ELD Systems** - Integrate with Samsara, Motive, or other tracking systems
    
    <Note>
      **Admin/Owner Only:** ELD system integrations can only be managed by users with Admin or Owner roles.
    </Note>
    
    <Tip>
      **Quick Access:** Press `Ctrl+K` (or `Cmd+K` on Mac) to open the command palette:
      
      <Frame>
        <img src="/assets/quickstart/command_prompt.png" alt="Command palette" />
      </Frame>
      
      Then type "integrations" to jump directly to the integrations page:
      
      <Frame>
        <img src="/assets/quickstart/jump_to.png" alt="Search for integrations" />
      </Frame>
    </Tip>
    
    **Or navigate manually:**
    
    1. Click your user dropdown at the bottom left
    
    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/quickstart/user_dropdown.png" alt="User dropdown menu" />
    </Frame>
    
    2. Select **Settings**
    
    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/quickstart/settings.png" alt="Settings menu" />
    </Frame>
    
    3. Click the **Integrations** tab
    
    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/quickstart/switch_to_integrations.png" alt="Switch to integrations tab" />
    </Frame>
    
    4. Connect your integrations
    
    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/quickstart/integrations_page_preview.png" alt="Integrations page" />
    </Frame>
    
    [View Integrations Guide](/integrations/overview)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Organize Your View">
    Group your dashboard by dispatchers to focus on what matters.
    
    Click the filters/groups icon in the top toolbar:
    
    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/quickstart/filters_groups.png" alt="Filters and groups toolbar" />
    </Frame>
    
    Select **Group by Dispatcher** from the dropdown:
    
    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/quickstart/groups_dropdown.png" alt="Groups dropdown menu" />
    </Frame>
    
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Dispatcher Grouping">
        Loads are organized with collapsible sections for each dispatcher, making it easy to focus on one dispatcher at a time:
        
        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/quickstart/group_by_dispatcher.png" alt="Loads grouped by dispatcher with collapsibles" />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Mixed Grouping">
        All loads are sorted by dispatcher but displayed in a continuous list without collapsible sections:
        
        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/quickstart/mixed_grouping.png" alt="Loads sorted by dispatcher in mixed view" />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
    
    <Tip>
      You can also apply filters and different grouping options to customize your view exactly how you need it.
    </Tip>
    
    Learn more about [Layout Customization](/customize/layout-customization) and [Organization](/customize/organization).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Your First Load">
    Ready to start dispatching? Create a load in seconds:
    
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Keyboard Shortcut">
        Press `Ctrl+L` (or `Cmd+L` on Mac) to open the load creation panel from anywhere.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Manual Navigation">
        Navigate to the Dispatch Board and click the **New Load** button:
        
        <Frame>
          <img src="/assets/quickstart/new_load_button.png" alt="New Load button" />
        </Frame>
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
    
    The load creation form will open:
    
    <Frame>
      <img src="/assets/quickstart/new_load_form.png" alt="Create new load form" />
    </Frame>
    
    You can create loads:
    - **Automatically** - AI extracts load details from rate confirmations
    - **Manually** - Enter load information step by step
    
    [Learn about Load Management](/management/load-management)
  </Step>
</Steps>

| `Ctrl+L` / `Cmd+L` | Create new load |
